## Ingredients
- 1/2 cup rolled oats
- 1/2 cup milk (any kind)
- 1/4 cup Greek yoghurt
- 1 tsp chia seeds
- 1 tsp honey or maple syrup
- tiny pinch of salt
- splash of vanilla extract (optional)

**Toppings**
- berries, sliced banana, a spoon of nut butter, or a sprinkle of cinnamon

## Method
1. In a jar or container, mix the oats, milk, yoghurt, chia seeds, honey, salt and vanilla.
2. Stir well, close the lid and refrigerate overnight (at least 6 hours).
3. In the morning, give it a stir. Add a splash more milk if it's too thick.
4. Add your toppings and eat cold, or warm it in the microwave for 1 minute.

## Notes
Not a fan of the oat texture? Blend everything until smooth before chilling for a creamy, pudding-like version. It keeps for up to 3 days in the fridge.
