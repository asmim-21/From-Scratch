## Ingredients
- 3 tbsp sooji (fine semolina)
- 2 tbsp ghee
- 3 tbsp sugar
- 1/2 cup water (or half water, half milk for a richer halwa)
- pinch of cardamom powder
- 1 tbsp chopped cashews and almonds
- a few raisins

## Method
1. In a small saucepan, heat the water (or water and milk) with the sugar until the sugar dissolves. Keep it hot on low heat.
2. In another pan, melt the ghee over low-medium heat. Add the nuts and raisins and fry for 1 minute until golden. Lift them out.
3. Add the sooji to the ghee and roast, stirring constantly, for 5–6 minutes until it turns light golden and smells nutty. Don't rush this step.
4. Carefully pour in the hot sugar water. It will splutter, so stand back. Stir quickly so no lumps form.
5. Keep stirring for 2–3 minutes until the sooji absorbs the liquid and the halwa leaves the sides of the pan.
6. Stir in the cardamom and most of the fried nuts. Serve warm, topped with the rest.

## Notes
The ratio to remember is 1 part sooji : 1 part sugar : 3–4 parts liquid. Double everything to share.
