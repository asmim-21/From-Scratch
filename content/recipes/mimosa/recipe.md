## Ingredients
- 75 ml chilled sparkling wine (prosecco, cava or champagne)
- 75 ml chilled fresh orange juice (pulp-free)
- orange slice or a strawberry, to garnish (optional)

## Method
1. Make sure both the sparkling wine and orange juice are very cold.
2. Tilt a champagne flute and pour in the sparkling wine first, slowly, so it doesn't foam over.
3. Gently top up with the orange juice.
4. Garnish with an orange slice or a strawberry on the rim.

## Notes
Don't stir, because the bubbles mix it for you. Swap the orange for pink grapefruit or peach juice for a twist.
