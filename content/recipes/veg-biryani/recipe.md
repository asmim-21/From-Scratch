## Ingredients
- 1/2 cup basmati rice
- 1 tbsp ghee or oil
- 1/2 onion, thinly sliced
- 1 bay leaf, 2 cloves, 2 green cardamom pods, 1 small piece cinnamon
- 1/2 tsp cumin seeds
- 1 tsp ginger-garlic paste
- 1 cup mixed vegetables (carrot, beans, peas, cauliflower, potato), chopped small
- 2 tbsp plain yoghurt
- 1/2 tsp biryani masala (or garam masala)
- 1/4 tsp turmeric
- 1/4 tsp red chilli powder
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1 cup water
- small handful of fresh mint and coriander, chopped
- pinch of saffron soaked in 1 tbsp warm milk (optional)

## Method
1. Rinse the rice 3–4 times until the water runs clear, then soak for 20 minutes. Drain.
2. Heat the ghee in a small heavy pot over medium heat. Add the whole spices and cumin seeds and let them sizzle for 30 seconds.
3. Add the onion and cook for 6–8 minutes until deep golden. Take out a spoonful of the onions for garnish.
4. Add the ginger-garlic paste and cook for 30 seconds. Add the vegetables, turmeric, chilli powder, biryani masala and salt, and stir for 2 minutes.
5. Turn the heat to low, stir in the yoghurt and half the mint and coriander, and cook for 2 minutes.
6. Add the drained rice and stir gently. Pour in 1 cup water and bring to a boil.
7. Cover tightly and cook on the lowest heat for 12–14 minutes. Don't lift the lid.
8. Turn off the heat, drizzle over the saffron milk, and rest covered for 5 minutes.
9. Fluff gently with a fork. Top with the fried onions and the remaining herbs.

## Notes
Serve with cucumber raita: plain yoghurt, grated cucumber, a pinch of salt and roasted cumin.
