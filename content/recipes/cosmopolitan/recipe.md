## Ingredients
- 45 ml citrus vodka (or plain vodka)
- 15 ml Cointreau (or triple sec)
- 30 ml cranberry juice
- 15 ml fresh lime juice
- ice
- strip of orange or lime peel, to garnish

## Method
1. Chill a martini or coupe glass by filling it with ice and water while you mix.
2. Fill a cocktail shaker with ice. Add the vodka, Cointreau, cranberry juice and lime juice.
3. Shake hard for 15 seconds until the shaker feels very cold.
4. Tip the ice water out of the glass, then strain the drink in.
5. Twist the peel over the drink to release its oils, then drop it in or hang it on the rim.

## Notes
No shaker? Use a jar with a tight lid. It should be a pale pink, not deep red, so go easy on the cranberry.
