## Ingredients
**Quick dough (one 20 cm pizza)**
- 1/2 cup self-raising flour, plus extra for dusting
- 1/4 cup Greek yoghurt
- pinch of salt

**Topping**
- 2 tbsp pizza sauce or passata
- pinch of dried oregano
- 1/2 cup grated mozzarella
- 1 tbsp grated parmesan (optional)
- drizzle of olive oil
- fresh basil leaves (optional)

## Method
1. Preheat the oven to 230°C (or as hot as it goes). Put a tray in the oven to heat up.
2. Mix the flour, yoghurt and salt into a soft dough. Knead on a floured bench for 1 minute until smooth.
3. Roll it out into a thin 20 cm circle on a sheet of baking paper.
4. Spread the sauce over the base, leaving a 1 cm border. Sprinkle with oregano, then the mozzarella and parmesan. Drizzle with olive oil.
5. Slide the pizza (still on its baking paper) onto the hot tray.
6. Bake for 10–12 minutes until the crust is golden and the cheese is bubbling with brown spots.
7. Top with fresh basil, slice and eat straight away.

## Notes
No passata? Mix 1 tbsp tomato paste with 1 tbsp water, a pinch of salt and oregano.
