## Ingredients (makes 2 muffins)
- 1/3 cup plain flour
- 2 tbsp sugar, plus a pinch for the top
- 1/2 tsp baking powder
- pinch of salt
- 1 tbsp melted butter
- 2 tbsp milk
- 1 tbsp beaten egg (beat 1 egg and use about a third)
- 1/4 tsp vanilla extract
- 1/4 cup blueberries (fresh or frozen)

## Method
1. Preheat the oven to 190°C. Line 2 holes of a muffin tin with paper cases.
2. In a bowl, mix the flour, sugar, baking powder and salt.
3. In a cup, mix the melted butter, milk, egg and vanilla.
4. Pour the wet mix into the dry and stir with a fork **just until combined**. A few lumps are fine; overmixing makes muffins tough.
5. Gently fold in the blueberries.
6. Spoon into the cases until about 2/3 full and sprinkle the tops with a little sugar.
7. Bake for 18–20 minutes, until golden and a toothpick poked into the middle comes out clean.
8. Cool for 5 minutes before eating.

## Notes
If using frozen blueberries, add them straight from the freezer (don't thaw) so the batter doesn't turn purple.
