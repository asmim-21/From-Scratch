## Ingredients
- 200 g spaghetti
- 3 tbsp olive oil, plus extra to finish
- 2 cloves garlic, lightly crushed
- 1 tin (400 g) whole peeled tomatoes
- a small bunch of basil
- salt
- a vegetarian hard cheese, to serve

## Method
1. Crush the tomatoes by hand in a bowl.
2. Warm the oil and garlic in a wide pan over a low heat for 3 minutes, until it smells lovely.
3. Add the tomatoes, a basil stalk and a good pinch of salt. Simmer for 15 to 20 minutes, then take out the garlic and basil.
4. Cook the spaghetti in salty water until 2 minutes before the packet time. Save a cup of the pasta water.
5. Move the spaghetti to the sauce with a splash of pasta water. Toss over a high heat until glossy.
6. Take off the heat, tear in the basil and add a little more oil. Serve with the cheese.
