## Ingredients
- 100 g spaghetti
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 2 cloves garlic, thinly sliced
- pinch of chilli flakes (optional)
- 200 g canned crushed tomatoes (half a 400 g can)
- 1/4 tsp salt, plus more for the pasta water
- pinch of sugar
- small knob of butter
- a few fresh basil leaves
- grated parmesan, to serve

## Method
1. Bring a pot of water to the boil and salt it generously (it should taste like the sea).
2. Meanwhile, heat the olive oil in a pan over medium-low heat. Add the garlic and chilli flakes and cook gently for 1–2 minutes until the garlic is pale golden. Don't let it burn.
3. Add the tomatoes, salt and sugar. Simmer for 10–12 minutes until slightly thickened.
4. Cook the spaghetti for 1 minute less than the packet says. Before draining, scoop out 1/4 cup of the pasta water.
5. Add the spaghetti straight into the sauce with a splash of pasta water and the butter. Toss over the heat for 1 minute until the sauce coats every strand and looks glossy.
6. Tear in the basil and serve topped with parmesan.

## Notes
The starchy pasta water is the secret to a silky sauce that clings to the pasta. Always save some.
