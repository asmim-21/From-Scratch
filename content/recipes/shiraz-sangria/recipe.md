## Ingredients
- 120 ml shiraz
- 15 ml brandy (optional)
- 15 ml orange juice
- 1 tsp honey or sugar syrup
- 2 orange slices, 2 apple slices, a few berries
- 30 ml soda water or lemonade
- ice
- cinnamon stick (optional)

## Method
1. In a glass or small jug, stir together the shiraz, brandy, orange juice and honey until the honey dissolves.
2. Add the fruit and lightly press it with a spoon to release its juices.
3. Let it sit for at least 10 minutes (or up to a few hours in the fridge) so the fruit soaks up the flavours.
4. Fill a wine glass with ice, pour in the sangria with the fruit, and top with soda water or lemonade.
5. Garnish with a cinnamon stick or an orange slice.

## Notes
Shiraz's bold, jammy flavour is perfect for sangria. A cheap bottle works just as well here.
