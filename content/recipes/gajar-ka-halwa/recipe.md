## Ingredients
- 2 medium carrots (about 200 g), peeled and finely grated
- 1 tbsp ghee
- 3/4 cup full-cream milk
- 2 tbsp sugar, or to taste
- 1 tbsp milk powder or crumbled khoya (optional, for richness)
- 1/4 tsp cardamom powder
- 1 tbsp chopped nuts (almonds, cashews, pistachios)
- a few raisins (optional)

## Method
1. Heat the ghee in a small heavy pan over medium heat. Add the grated carrots and sauté for 5 minutes, until they soften and smell sweet.
2. Pour in the milk and bring to a boil, then lower the heat to a steady simmer.
3. Cook for 20–25 minutes, stirring every few minutes, until almost all the milk has been absorbed.
4. Add the sugar and stir. It will become a little runny again. Keep cooking for 5–8 minutes until it thickens and leaves the sides of the pan.
5. Stir in the milk powder or khoya (if using), the cardamom, raisins and most of the nuts. Cook for 1–2 more minutes.
6. Serve warm, topped with the remaining nuts.

## Notes
Red Delhi carrots give the classic colour, but regular carrots work perfectly. Also delicious with a scoop of vanilla ice cream.
