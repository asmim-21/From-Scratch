## Ingredients
- 1 tbsp butter or olive oil
- 1/2 small onion, chopped
- 1 clove garlic, chopped
- 400 g can whole or crushed tomatoes
- 1/2 cup vegetable stock (or water)
- 1/2 tsp sugar
- 1/4 tsp salt and a pinch of pepper
- 2 tbsp cream, plus a little to serve
- a few basil leaves (optional)

## Method
1. Melt the butter in a small pot over medium heat. Add the onion and cook for 5 minutes until soft and see-through.
2. Add the garlic and cook for 30 seconds.
3. Add the tomatoes, stock, sugar, salt and pepper. Bring to a boil, then simmer for 15 minutes.
4. Blend until completely smooth, using a stick blender in the pot or carefully in a blender (let it cool slightly first and hold the lid down with a tea towel).
5. Stir in the cream and basil. Taste and adjust the salt.
6. Serve with a swirl of cream on top.

## Notes
Dunk a grilled cheese sandwich in it, which is the classic combination.
