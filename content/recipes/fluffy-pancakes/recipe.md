## Ingredients (makes 3 pancakes)
- 1/2 cup plain flour
- 1 tbsp sugar
- 1 tsp baking powder
- pinch of salt
- 1 egg
- 1/3 cup milk
- 1 tbsp melted butter, plus extra for the pan
- 1/4 tsp vanilla extract

**To serve**
- maple syrup, berries, sliced banana, a dusting of icing sugar

## Method
1. In a bowl, whisk the flour, sugar, baking powder and salt.
2. In another bowl, whisk the egg, milk, melted butter and vanilla.
3. Pour the wet into the dry and stir gently until just combined. A few small lumps are good. Rest the batter for 5 minutes.
4. Heat a non-stick pan over medium-low heat and add a little butter.
5. Pour in about 1/4 cup of batter per pancake. Cook for 2–3 minutes until bubbles form on top and the edges look set.
6. Flip and cook for 1–2 minutes more until golden.
7. Stack them up and add your toppings.

## Notes
The first pancake is always a test, so adjust the heat if it browns too fast or too slow.
