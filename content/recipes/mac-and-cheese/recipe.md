## Ingredients
- 3/4 cup macaroni (about 80 g)
- 1 tbsp butter
- 1 tbsp plain flour
- 3/4 cup milk
- 3/4 cup grated cheddar (tasty cheese)
- 2 tbsp grated parmesan or mozzarella
- 1/4 tsp mustard (Dijon or American), optional
- pinch of salt, pepper and paprika

## Method
1. Cook the macaroni in salted boiling water until just tender. Drain.
2. In the same pot, melt the butter over medium heat. Whisk in the flour and cook for 1 minute, stirring, until it smells toasty.
3. Slowly pour in the milk, whisking the whole time so there are no lumps. Cook for 2–3 minutes until it thickens enough to coat a spoon.
4. Turn the heat to low. Stir in the cheeses and mustard until melted and smooth. Season with salt and pepper.
5. Add the macaroni and stir to coat. Add a splash more milk if it's too thick.
6. Serve with a pinch of paprika on top.

## Notes
For a crunchy top, put it in an ovenproof dish, sprinkle with breadcrumbs and extra cheese, and grill for 3–4 minutes until golden.
