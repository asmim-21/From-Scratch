## Ingredients
- 1/2 cup plain flour (all-purpose), plus extra for dusting
- 1/4 tsp baking powder
- pinch of baking soda
- 1/4 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp sugar
- 3 tbsp plain yoghurt
- 1–2 tbsp warm milk
- 1 tsp oil
- 1 tbsp butter, melted
- pinch of chopped coriander or nigella seeds (optional)

## Method
1. In a bowl, mix the flour, baking powder, baking soda, salt and sugar.
2. Add the yoghurt and oil, then add the milk a little at a time and knead into a soft, slightly sticky dough (2–3 minutes).
3. Cover and rest for at least 15 minutes.
4. Divide into 2 balls. Roll each into an oval about 5 mm thick, dusting with flour. Press a few coriander leaves or nigella seeds into the top if using.
5. Heat a heavy pan (cast iron is best) over medium-high heat until very hot.
6. Brush a little water on one side of the naan and place it water-side down in the pan. Cook for about 1 minute until bubbles appear on top.
7. Flip and cook the other side for 30–60 seconds until golden brown spots form. You can also hold it over a gas flame with tongs for a few seconds to char it.
8. Brush generously with melted butter and serve hot.

## Notes
The water on the first side helps the naan stick to the pan so it puffs up. Makes 2 small naans.
