## Ingredients
- 1 portion hakka or egg-free noodles (about 80 g dried)
- 1 tbsp oil
- 2 cloves garlic, finely chopped
- 1/2 small onion, thinly sliced
- 1/2 cup cabbage, finely shredded
- 1/4 capsicum, thinly sliced
- 1/2 carrot, cut into thin matchsticks
- 1 spring onion, sliced (whites and greens separated)
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tsp vinegar
- 1/2 tsp chilli sauce
- 1/2 tsp tomato ketchup (optional)
- pinch of black pepper and salt

## Method
1. Cook the noodles in salted boiling water according to the packet, until just tender. Drain, rinse under cold water and toss with a few drops of oil so they don't stick.
2. Heat the oil in a wok or large pan over **high** heat until smoking.
3. Add the garlic and spring onion whites and stir-fry for 20 seconds.
4. Add the onion, carrot, cabbage and capsicum. Stir-fry for 2 minutes. The vegetables should stay crunchy.
5. Add the noodles, soy sauce, vinegar, chilli sauce, ketchup, pepper and salt. Toss everything together with tongs for 1–2 minutes over high heat.
6. Top with the spring onion greens and serve hot.

## Notes
Keep the heat high and keep everything moving, because that's what gives it the street-style smoky flavour. Add shredded cooked chicken in step 5 for chicken chowmein.
