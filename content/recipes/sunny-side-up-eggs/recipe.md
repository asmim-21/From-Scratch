## Ingredients
- 2 eggs
- 1 tsp butter or oil
- pinch of salt and black pepper
- chilli flakes or chopped chives (optional)
- toast, to serve

## Method
1. Heat a non-stick pan over **medium-low** heat and melt the butter so it coats the base.
2. Crack each egg into a small bowl first (so no shell gets in), then gently slide it into the pan.
3. Cook without touching for 3–4 minutes, until the whites are completely set and the yolk is still runny.
4. If the whites around the yolk are still a bit clear, cover the pan with a lid for 30–60 seconds. The steam sets them without flipping.
5. Slide onto a plate. Season with salt, pepper and chilli flakes.

## Notes
Low heat gives tender whites with no crispy brown edges. Turn the heat up a little if you like lacy, crispy edges.
