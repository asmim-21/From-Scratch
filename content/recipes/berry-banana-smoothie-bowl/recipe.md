## Ingredients
- 1 cup frozen mixed berries
- 1 frozen banana, sliced (peel it before freezing)
- 1/4 cup milk or plant milk (add slowly)
- 2 tbsp Greek yoghurt (optional, for creaminess)
- 1 tsp honey (optional)

**Toppings**
- fresh banana slices
- fresh berries
- granola
- chia seeds, coconut flakes or a drizzle of nut butter

## Method
1. Put the frozen berries, frozen banana, yoghurt and honey into a blender.
2. Add just 2–3 tbsp of the milk to start. Blend, stopping to scrape down the sides and push everything towards the blades.
3. Add more milk a tablespoon at a time only if needed. You want it thick enough to eat with a spoon, like soft-serve.
4. Scoop into a bowl and arrange your toppings in neat rows.
5. Eat straight away before it melts.

## Notes
The less liquid you use, the thicker the bowl. Keep a bag of sliced bananas in the freezer so it's always ready.
