## Ingredients
- 90 ml prosecco
- 60 ml Aperol
- 30 ml soda water
- lots of ice
- orange slice, to garnish

## Method
1. Fill a large wine glass with ice.
2. Pour in the prosecco first, then the Aperol.
3. Top with the soda water.
4. Give it one gentle stir and add the orange slice.

## Notes
The easy ratio to remember is 3-2-1: three parts prosecco, two parts Aperol, one part soda.
