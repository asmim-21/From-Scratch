## Ingredients
**Chicken marinade**
- 150 g chicken thigh, bone-in or boneless, cut into pieces
- 3 tbsp plain yoghurt
- 1 tsp ginger-garlic paste
- 1/2 tsp biryani masala (or garam masala)
- 1/4 tsp turmeric
- 1/2 tsp red chilli powder
- 1/2 tsp salt
- squeeze of lemon juice

**Rice**
- 1/2 cup basmati rice
- 1 bay leaf, 2 cloves, 2 green cardamom pods, 1 small piece cinnamon
- 1/2 tsp salt

**To layer**
- 1 tbsp ghee or oil
- 1 onion, thinly sliced
- small handful of fresh mint and coriander, chopped
- pinch of saffron soaked in 1 tbsp warm milk

## Method
1. Mix the chicken with all the marinade ingredients. Cover and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes (or overnight for the best flavour).
2. Rinse the rice until the water runs clear and soak for 20 minutes.
3. Heat the ghee in a small heavy pot and fry the onion for 8–10 minutes until deep brown and crispy. Lift out half for topping.
4. Add the chicken and all its marinade to the pot. Cook on medium for 8–10 minutes, stirring, until the chicken is nearly cooked and the sauce is thick.
5. Meanwhile, boil a pot of water with the whole spices and salt. Add the drained rice and cook for 5 minutes, until it's about 70% done (it should still have a firm bite). Drain.
6. Spread the half-cooked rice over the chicken. Scatter over the mint, coriander and the crispy onions, then drizzle with the saffron milk.
7. Cover with a tight lid (seal with foil underneath if it's loose) and cook on the lowest heat for 15–18 minutes. This is the "dum".
8. Rest for 5 minutes, then gently mix from the bottom up as you serve, so you get chicken and rice in every scoop.

## Notes
The chicken must be cooked all the way through: no pink inside, and the juices run clear. Serve with raita and a wedge of lemon.
