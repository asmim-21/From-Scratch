## Ingredients
**Sugar syrup**
- 1/2 cup sugar
- 1/2 cup water
- 2 green cardamom pods, lightly crushed
- 1/2 tsp rose water
- few strands of saffron (optional)

**Jamuns (makes 6 small)**
- 1/4 cup milk powder
- 1 tbsp plain flour (maida)
- pinch of baking soda
- 1 tsp ghee
- 1–2 tbsp milk
- oil or ghee for deep frying

## Method
1. **Syrup:** Simmer the sugar, water and cardamom for 5 minutes until slightly sticky (not stringy). Turn off the heat and stir in the rose water and saffron. Keep warm.
2. **Dough:** Mix the milk powder, flour and baking soda. Rub in the ghee, then add milk a teaspoon at a time and gently bring it together into a soft, smooth dough. Don't knead hard.
3. Rest the dough for 5 minutes, then roll into 6 small balls. They should be completely smooth with no cracks, or they'll split while frying.
4. Heat oil in a small pan over **low** heat. To test, drop in a tiny piece of dough. It should rise slowly with small bubbles, not sizzle wildly.
5. Fry the balls on low heat for 6–8 minutes, gently turning them, until they're an even deep golden brown all over.
6. Drain and drop them straight into the warm syrup. Soak for at least 30 minutes; they'll swell and soften.

## Notes
Low heat is everything: if the oil is too hot, the outside browns while the inside stays raw. Serve warm, on their own or with vanilla ice cream.
