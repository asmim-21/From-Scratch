## Ingredients
- 1 medium potato, peeled and chopped
- 1/4 cup cauliflower florets
- 2 tbsp green peas
- 1/4 cup water
- 2 tbsp butter, plus extra for the pav
- 1/2 small onion, finely chopped, plus extra raw onion to serve
- 1/4 capsicum, finely chopped
- 1 tsp ginger-garlic paste
- 1 tomato, finely chopped
- 1 1/2 tsp pav bhaji masala
- 1/4 tsp Kashmiri chilli powder
- 1/2 tsp salt
- squeeze of lemon
- chopped coriander
- 2 pav (soft dinner rolls)

## Method
1. Boil or pressure cook the potato, cauliflower and peas in 1/4 cup water until very soft (about 10 minutes, or 3 whistles). Mash well.
2. Melt 1 tbsp butter in a pan over medium heat. Add the onion and cook for 3 minutes, then add the capsicum and ginger-garlic paste and cook for 2 more minutes.
3. Add the tomato, pav bhaji masala, chilli powder and salt. Cook for 5 minutes, mashing the tomato as it softens.
4. Add the mashed vegetables and a splash of water. Mash everything together with a potato masher right in the pan.
5. Simmer for 5–8 minutes, mashing and stirring, until thick and saucy. Stir in the remaining 1 tbsp butter, lemon juice and coriander.
6. Slice the pav in half horizontally. Toast in a hot pan with butter and a pinch of pav bhaji masala until crisp and golden.
7. Serve the bhaji topped with a cube of butter, raw onion, coriander and a lemon wedge, with the hot pav on the side.

## Notes
Use lots of butter, because that's the Mumbai way.
