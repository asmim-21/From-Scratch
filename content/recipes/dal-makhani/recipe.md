## Ingredients
- 1/4 cup whole black urad dal
- 1 tbsp red kidney beans (rajma)
- 1 1/2 cups water, plus extra for soaking
- 1 tbsp butter
- 1/2 small onion, finely chopped
- 1 tsp ginger-garlic paste
- 1/2 cup tomato puree
- 1/4 tsp red chilli powder (Kashmiri if you have it)
- 1/4 tsp garam masala
- 1/4 tsp salt, or to taste
- 2 tbsp thickened cream
- 1/4 tsp kasuri methi (dried fenugreek leaves), crushed

## Method
1. Rinse the urad dal and rajma, then soak them in plenty of water overnight (8 hours).
2. Drain, add 1 1/2 cups fresh water and a pinch of salt, and pressure cook for 6–7 whistles (about 25 minutes). The lentils should be completely soft and mash easily. No pressure cooker? Simmer covered for about 1 1/2 hours, topping up with water.
3. Lightly mash about a third of the dal with the back of a spoon.
4. In a separate pan, melt the butter over medium heat. Add the onion and cook for 4–5 minutes until golden.
5. Add the ginger-garlic paste and cook for 30 seconds, then add the tomato puree, chilli powder and salt. Cook for 5–6 minutes until the butter starts to separate at the edges.
6. Pour in the cooked dal and stir. Simmer on low for 15–20 minutes, stirring now and then and adding a splash of water if it gets too thick. The longer it simmers, the creamier it gets.
7. Stir in the garam masala, kasuri methi and cream. Simmer for 2 more minutes.

## Notes
Serve with butter naan or jeera rice. Finish with an extra swirl of cream and a small knob of butter on top.
