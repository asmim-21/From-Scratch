## Ingredients
- 45 ml vodka
- 120 ml tomato juice
- 10 ml fresh lemon juice
- 2 dashes Worcestershire sauce
- 2 dashes Tabasco (or more if you like it spicy)
- pinch of celery salt and black pepper
- 1/4 tsp horseradish (optional)
- ice
- celery stick and lemon wedge, to garnish

## Method
1. Fill a tall glass with ice.
2. In a separate glass or jug, stir together the vodka, tomato juice, lemon juice, Worcestershire, Tabasco, celery salt, pepper and horseradish.
3. Pour it back and forth between two glasses a few times to mix it without making it watery.
4. Pour over the ice in the tall glass.
5. Garnish with a celery stick and a lemon wedge.

## Notes
Worcestershire sauce usually contains anchovies, so look for a vegetarian version if needed. Rim the glass with celery salt for extra flair.
