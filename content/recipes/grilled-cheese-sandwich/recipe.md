## Ingredients
- 2 slices bread (sourdough or white)
- 1 tbsp soft butter
- 2 slices cheddar, or 1/2 cup grated cheddar
- 2 tbsp grated mozzarella (for extra stretch)

## Method
1. Butter one side of each slice of bread, right to the edges.
2. Place one slice butter-side down in a cold frying pan. Pile on the cheddar and mozzarella, then top with the second slice, butter-side up.
3. Turn the heat to medium-low. Cook for 3–4 minutes, pressing down gently with a spatula, until the bottom is deep golden.
4. Flip carefully and cook for 3 more minutes until golden and the cheese has completely melted.
5. Rest for 1 minute, then cut diagonally.

## Notes
Low and slow is the trick, so the cheese melts before the bread burns. Perfect with the tomato soup.
