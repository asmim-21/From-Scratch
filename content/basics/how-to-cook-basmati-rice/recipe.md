---
title: How to cook basmati rice
tags: [rice, foundations]
---

Separate, fluffy grains every time. The trick is rinsing, soaking, and leaving the lid alone.

## What you need
- 1 cup basmati rice
- 1½ cups water
- ½ tsp salt
- 1 tsp ghee or butter (optional)

## Steps
1. Rinse the rice in 4 or 5 changes of water, until the water is nearly clear.
2. Soak it in cold water for 20 minutes, then drain well.
3. Bring the rice, water, salt and ghee to the boil in a pot with a tight lid.
4. Turn the heat to the lowest setting, cover, and cook for 10 minutes. Don't lift the lid.
5. Take it off the heat and leave it covered for another 10 minutes.
6. Fluff with a fork.

## Notes
- 1 cup of dry rice feeds about 3 people.
- If it's still wet at the end, put the lid back on and wait 5 more minutes.
